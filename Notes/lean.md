# Lean Product Management Notes

The inspiration for these notes comes from the book *Lean Product Playbook.  How to Innovate with Minimum Viable Products and Rapid Customer Feedback* by Dan Olsen.

## Key Concepts

- **Lean Product Development**: Focus on delivering value to customers quickly and efficiently.
- **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**: A product with just enough features to satisfy early adopters and gather feedback.
- **Customer Feedback**: Continuous feedback from users is essential for product improvement.

## Principles

1. **Build-Measure-Learn**: A cycle for validating ideas and learning from users.
2. **Hypothesis-Driven Development**: Formulate hypotheses about user needs and test them through experiments.
3. **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly iterate on the product based on user feedback and changing market conditions.

## Practices

- **User Interviews**: Conduct interviews to understand user needs and pain points.
- **Prototyping**: Create low-fidelity prototypes to test ideas quickly.
- **A/B Testing**: Experiment with different versions of a product to determine which performs better.

## Conclusion

Lean product management emphasizes the importance of agility, customer feedback, and iterative development in creating successful products.
